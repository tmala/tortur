Privacy Policy for Tortur

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tortur ("we", "our", or "the app"), hosted at https://trillebar.no/tortur, handles your information.

Tortur is a private, non-commercial app intended exclusively for an authorized group of personal contacts.

1. Information We Collect

To operate the service and manage private access, we collect and process minimal personal information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture (via standard openid, email, and profile scopes).
  • Vandringer (Tracks): The track from your device if you select to upload the tracks, including images you include and the description you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Authentication & Access Control: To verify your identity and match your Google email address against a private, pre-approved whitelist of authorized users. If your email is not on this list, access is denied.
  • Service Functionality: To display your name, profile picture, and check-ins (Turpost) including the track for this hike.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, tracking, or profiling.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

SnikkSnakk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Chat Data: Tracking of check-ins (Turpost) and basic user identifiers are stored securely only for as long as necessary to provide the ongoing check-in functionality.
  • Access Revocation: You can revoke TorTur's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Data Deletion: If you wish to have your past messages or user details removed from the whitelist/database, please contact the developer directly.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please contact:
